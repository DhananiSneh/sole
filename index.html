One founder

An IT company with two lines.

You bring the work. The teams carry it. Services is software for a client. Products is software you own.

Services

Ends in an offer, a page, and an invoice you send.

  1. Desk Opens the client lead.
  2. Sales Writes the offer and the price.
  3. Delivery Builds what the client asked for.
  4. Accounts Invoices that price.
  5. Review Can hold the folder.
node src/cli.js service "A clinic needs a booking site. Budget 80000 INR. Due in 3 weeks."

Products

Ends in a spec, a product, and a price you ship.

  1. Desk Opens the product brief.
  2. Product Writes what version one includes.
  3. Engineering Builds the product.
  4. Accounts Sets the customer price.
  5. Review Can hold the ship.
node src/cli.js product "Ship an invoicing tool for freelancers. Price 499 INR a month."

The writing teams use Grok when XAI_API_KEY is set. Without a key, the same paths run locally. Source
